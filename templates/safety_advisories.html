Vaccination & Health Clearance

Ensure all standard routine immunizations are up to date prior to international travel. Certain tropical destinations require Yellow Fever or Hepatitis clearance certificate upon arrival.

  • Carry physical and digital copies of vaccination records.
  • Pack a personal prescription medical kit with original pharmacy labels.
